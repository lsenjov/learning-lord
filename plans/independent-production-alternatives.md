# Independent production alternatives

Each profitable recipe independently targets up to twelve hours of production at current skill. Carried, listed and underway output reduce its stock gap; recent own sales plus unmet demand can raise desired stock. Calculate each alternative against the same current funds and inputs, reserving inputs committed to an active batch. Completed daily work does not reduce these future alternatives. Ingredient reserves and standing purchase demand use the maximum per-good requirement across recipes, rather than summing alternatives. Remove an underway batch from its recipe's future count before taking maxima, then add its committed inputs separately. Personal food reserves use inventory remaining after production ingredients; purchase shortages deduct carried and owned listed stock.

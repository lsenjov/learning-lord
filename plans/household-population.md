# Household population and property

## Scope

Implement units 1-4, then stop for user inspection. Each unit receives an independent code review, resolution of valid high/medium findings followed by fresh review, appropriate validation, and its own commit. Fix low documentation findings; report other low findings. Keep the UI compact and put explanatory text in tooltips.

Old saves may be invalidated. Do not add compatibility migration. Stop for discussion if an unexpected complication materially changes behavior or architecture, or a small specification change would significantly simplify implementation.

## Authorized units

1. **Halve degradation rates.** Halve condition loss globally for buildings, built objects, and clothing, including usage wear. Do not alter needs growth, diet decay, construction speed, or repair speed. Check all condition-loss paths and update relevant expectations/documentation.
2. **Universal households and household ownership.** Every citizen in a universe belongs to exactly one nonempty household, including single citizens. Create a household with its first member, then add new citizens directly to that household during setup or live dev creation. Do not temporarily create separate households and merge them, or insert householdless citizens. General household merging and moving existing citizens between households are out of scope. Private buildings, built objects, funds, and stored goods belong to households; individual members still carry items and wear clothes. Preserve town/public and guild ownership. Update creation, economic access, histories, UI ownership display, and persistence validation consistently. Reject old save versions. Preserve existing simulation behavior apart from the agreed ownership change.
3. **Mixed residential/workplace buildings.** In new-world generation combine eligible private household workplaces and accommodation in one building. A weaver can live where they work. Keep guild premises and large/public workplaces such as fields and forests separate. Reuse existing rooms, object capacity, and location capabilities where possible; validate residents can sleep and perform their profession. Do not silently discard assets or create duplicate workplaces.
4a. **Citizens without accommodation and discomfort.** Household membership requires neither a home nor a bed. New households may be homeless; new members share any existing household home/supplies but insertion never automatically builds a bedroom or bed. Preserve deliberately furnished initial-world households through explicit setup. Homeless citizens carry goods and can eat and drink from carried supplies; planning and provisioning must work without home storage. Citizens without usable beds may sleep outdoors with the SAME tiredness recovery and duration as bed sleep. Add discomfort, bounded 0-100, subtracting one point per unit from wellbeing. Passive decay is 10 per simulation day. Eight hours outdoors adds 25 discomfort, eight hours in an actual usable bed removes 25. Apply sleep effects continuously proportional to elapsed sleep, simultaneously with passive decay, and clamp the combined result; interrupted sleep counts. Update planning/prediction, histories, compact need displays and save validation consistently. No inns, housing acquisition, or departure behavior yet.
4b. **Dev mode and citizen creation.** Add a local UI preference revealing compact dev menus. Add a newly created member to a selected household with a chosen existing profession, or create a homeless household with its first member's profession and chosen starting funds. Use household-first creation without merging existing agents. Do not create accommodation, workshops, tools, or guild membership implicitly. Created entities and funds persist in simulation saves independently of the toggle. Integrate through the simulation worker and refresh household/economic/history state safely. Review and commit 4a and 4b separately, then stop for user inspection.

## Validation

Use focused behavioral tests for each simulation change, formatting and relevant compilation checks, and broader existing tests where cross-cutting ownership or building changes warrant them. Check persistence round trips and rejection of old saves. Test new-world household/ownership invariants and mixed-use sleep/work access. Test homeless and bedless citizens, carried food/drink and replenishment, discomfort rates/bounds/partition invariance, live dev creation, and save/load. Record commands and results below as work proceeds.

## Deferred units

5. Household departure and dev removal of a member or whole household. At member planning boundaries sample average household wellbeing; below zero starts/continues a timer, zero or above clears it. Leave at the first below-zero sample after 24 hours. Departing households take all portable belongings and coins; buildings and built objects transfer to town. Dev removal bypasses the timer and uses departure cleanup. Individual removal's asset division remains to be settled.
6. Town grants and property listings: free grants, fixed prices, current value, or percentage discount from current value.
7. Inns: paid beds and meals for resident citizens; no travellers. Basic homelessness support is brought forward into 4a.
8. Town site creation and development: town chooses and pays for development before granting or listing property.
9. Autonomous purchase and development: only propertyless households buy; compare land or developed buildings according to needs and development costs, then build to suit. Land ownership alone does not provide housing.
